## Principles

The company reimburses costs that are necessary to do the work, reasonable in amount, and evidenced. The policy sets limits so that people do not have to guess, but a cost inside a limit is not automatically reimbursable and a cost slightly outside one is not automatically refused. Managers approve against the principle first and the limit second.

Employees should not be out of pocket for business costs, and should not profit from them either. Where a booking can be made by the company rather than paid personally and reclaimed, it should be. The company holds accounts with the travel agency and the main hotel chains for exactly this reason, and Finance would rather approve a corporate booking in advance than reimburse a personal card afterwards.

## Booking travel

All flights, rail and hotel bookings are made through the corporate travel agency or the online booking tool. Bookings made elsewhere are reimbursed only where the traveller can show the same itinerary was unavailable through the tool, or where the booking was made during a disruption and the tool was not reachable.

Travel is booked at least fourteen days in advance where the date is known. Late bookings need the cost centre owner's approval, recorded in the request. Economy class is the standard for all flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights over six hours, and business class only for flights over nine hours or where the traveller works the day of arrival. Rail is booked in standard class, and first class only where it is cheaper than standard at the time of booking, which on some routes it is.

## Accommodation and meals

Hotels are booked within the nightly caps published by Finance for each city, which are reviewed every six months. The cap is inclusive of local taxes and excludes breakfast where breakfast is separately charged. Where no room is available inside the cap, the traveller books the nearest reasonable alternative and notes it in the claim.

Meals are reimbursed against receipts up to 55 EUR per full day of travel, covering all meals in that day. There is no per diem: unspent allowance is not payable. Alcohol is not reimbursable except as part of a customer entertainment claim approved in advance. Where a meal is provided as part of a conference or by a customer, the daily limit reduces accordingly. Room service and minibar charges are treated as meals and count against the same limit.

## Ground transport

Public transport is the default. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable for journeys to and from airports and stations, for journeys with heavy equipment, for travel between 22:00 and 06:00, and where the traveller judges a route unsafe. A taxi taken for convenience during the working day on a route served by public transport is not reimbursable.

Private car use on company business is reimbursed at the statutory rate per kilometre in the country of travel, covering fuel, wear and insurance. Distance is claimed from the workplace or from home, whichever is shorter. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts. Fines of any kind, including parking and speeding, are never reimbursable and are not payable by the company even where they were incurred on company business in a company vehicle.

## Customer entertainment and gifts

Entertainment is reimbursable where an employee of the company is present, the business purpose is recorded and the guests are named in the claim. Claims listing only a company name rather than individuals will be returned. The limit is 90 EUR per head including drinks, and anything above it needs prior approval from the commercial director.

Gifts to customers must not exceed 50 EUR in value and must never be cash or a cash equivalent such as a voucher. Gifts to public officials are prohibited outright, in any amount, in any country, including hospitality that would be unremarkable in a commercial setting. Where a customer's own policy sets a lower limit than ours, the customer's limit applies. Anything offered to an employee that exceeds these limits is declined politely and reported to Compliance.

## Submitting and approving claims

Claims are submitted in the expense system within thirty days of the cost being incurred. Claims older than ninety days are not reimbursed except where the delay was caused by extended sick leave or parental leave. Each line carries a legible receipt; card statements are not receipts. For amounts under 20 EUR a receipt is not required, provided the line is described specifically.

The approver is the claimant's manager, and no one approves their own claim under any circumstance, including where the manager is absent. Where the manager is unavailable the claim goes to the manager's manager. Finance audits a random sample of ten percent of claims each month, plus every claim over 1,000 EUR. Reimbursement is made in the payroll run following approval.

## Getting it wrong

Most policy breaches are administrative: a missing receipt, a late claim, a booking made outside the tool. These are corrected by returning the claim with an explanation, and Finance is expected to explain rather than simply reject.

Deliberate misuse is treated differently. Claiming for costs not incurred, inflating amounts, splitting a claim to stay under an approval threshold, or claiming a personal cost as a business one is fraud, and is handled under the gross misconduct provisions of the employee handbook rather than under this policy. Anyone who suspects it should raise it through the confidential reporting line rather than approaching the individual, and they are protected from retaliation for having done so.
