## Parties and term

This agreement is between the company, registered in Rotterdam, and the Supplier, registered in Bergen, for the supply of corrugated packaging, pallet wrap and void fill to the company's terminals in the Netherlands, Poland and Canada.

The initial term is thirty-six months from 1 May 2026, renewing automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety days before the end of the then-current term. The agreement may be terminated for convenience by the company on one hundred and twenty days' notice after the first eighteen months. The Supplier has no equivalent right of termination for convenience during the initial term. Termination for cause is available to either party on thirty days' notice where a material breach is not remedied within that period.

## Products, pricing and price review

Pricing is fixed for the first twelve months at the rates set out in schedule A. Thereafter prices may be adjusted once per contract year, on the anniversary date, by no more than the change in the published index for containerboard over the preceding twelve months, or four percent, whichever is lower.

The Supplier gives written notice of any proposed adjustment at least sixty days before it takes effect, with the index data supporting it. The company may dispute the calculation within twenty days, and the parties will meet within ten working days of a dispute being raised. Prices are inclusive of standard packaging and labelling and exclusive of VAT. Prices are expressed in EUR for the Netherlands and Poland and in CAD for Canada, and currency movement is the Supplier's risk within the term.

## Ordering, lead times and delivery

The company places orders through electronic purchase orders against the agreed catalogue. The Supplier acknowledges each order within one working day and confirms the delivery date. The standard lead time is ten working days for catalogue items and twenty working days for items made to the company's specification.

Delivery is DAP to the named terminal under Incoterms 2020, during the receiving windows published for each site. The Supplier bears the cost and risk of delivery to that point. A delivery that arrives outside the receiving window may be refused and re-presented at the Supplier's cost. Partial deliveries are accepted only where agreed in advance for the specific order. Where the Supplier anticipates a delay it notifies the company's procurement contact as soon as it knows, and in any event before the confirmed delivery date rather than after it.

## Returns and rejections

The company may reject any delivery that does not conform to the specification, is damaged, or is short against the order. Returns are accepted within 48 hours of delivery for damage or shortage apparent on receipt, and within thirty days for latent defects not reasonably discoverable on inspection.

A rejection is notified in writing with the purchase order number, the quantity affected and photographs where the defect is visible. The Supplier collects rejected goods at its own cost within five working days of notification, or authorises disposal in writing. The company is not obliged to store rejected goods beyond ten working days. A credit note is issued within fifteen days of collection or authorised disposal. Replacement goods, where requested, are supplied at the original price and on the standard lead time, running from the date of the rejection notice.

## Service levels and remedies

The Supplier will achieve on-time in-full delivery of at least ninety-six percent, measured monthly across all sites against confirmed delivery dates. Performance is reviewed quarterly at a meeting attended by both parties' account managers.

Where performance falls below ninety-six percent in any month, the Supplier provides a written explanation and corrective plan within ten working days. Where it falls below ninety-two percent in two consecutive months, the company may apply a service credit of two percent of the affected month's invoiced value, and may source substitute supply from a third party and recover any excess cost from the Supplier. Service credits are the sole financial remedy for delivery failure, and do not limit the company's right to terminate for material breach where failures persist.

## Quality, compliance and audit

All corrugated material is supplied with a recycled content of at least seventy percent and carries FSC or PEFC chain-of-custody certification. The Supplier maintains ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification throughout the term and notifies the company within five working days of any lapse, suspension or adverse audit finding.

The Supplier warrants that no forced, bonded or child labour is used at any point in its supply chain, and that it complies with applicable sanctions and export control regimes. The company may audit the Supplier's production sites and records on twenty working days' notice, no more than twice a year, at its own cost, and may bring an independent auditor bound by the confidentiality terms of this agreement. The Supplier extends equivalent audit rights to its own subcontractors and makes the results available to the company on request.

## Liability, insurance and confidentiality

Each party's aggregate liability under this agreement is limited to the total charges paid in the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for a breach of the confidentiality obligations below.

The Supplier maintains product liability insurance of at least five million EUR per occurrence and provides a certificate annually. Each party keeps the other's commercial, technical and pricing information confidential during the term and for five years afterwards, and uses it only to perform this agreement. Neither party may name the other in marketing material without prior written consent. The agreement is governed by Dutch law, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Rotterdam.
