## Parties and scope of services

This agreement is between the company and the Supplier, registered in Dublin, for the provision of temperature-controlled storage and last-mile distribution of pharmaceutical and food consignments handled by the company in Ireland and the United Kingdom.

The Supplier provides storage in three temperature bands: ambient controlled at 15 to 25 degrees, chilled at 2 to 8 degrees, and frozen at minus 18 degrees or below. The scope covers receipt, put-away, storage, pick, pack and delivery to the consignee, together with the temperature records and chain-of-custody documentation that the consignments require. It does not cover customs clearance, which the company performs itself, nor return logistics, which are quoted separately per movement.

## Term, volumes and exclusivity

The term is twenty-four months from 1 March 2026, extendable by mutual written agreement. The company commits to a minimum monthly volume of 900 pallet positions across the three bands, calculated as a monthly average over each quarter rather than in any single month.

The company does not grant exclusivity. It may place temperature-controlled volume with other providers, and the Supplier acknowledges that the minimum commitment is the whole of the company's obligation. Where the company falls below the minimum in a quarter, it pays for the shortfall in pallet positions at the contracted storage rate. Where the company exceeds 1,400 positions in a month, the Supplier will accept the excess where space allows and may charge the published overflow rate, having given notice that overflow rates will apply before accepting the volume.

## Temperature integrity and excursion handling

The Supplier monitors every storage area continuously and every vehicle in transit, at intervals no greater than five minutes, with calibrated sensors whose calibration certificates are made available to the company annually. Records are retained for the shelf life of the product plus one year, and in no case less than three years.

A temperature excursion is any reading outside the contracted band for more than fifteen consecutive minutes. The Supplier notifies the company's duty controller within one hour of an excursion being detected, whatever the hour, with the location, duration and affected consignments. Product affected by an excursion is quarantined and not released until the company or the consignment owner gives written disposition. The Supplier does not make the disposition decision itself under any circumstances, including where the excursion is minor and the product is plainly unaffected.

## Delivery performance and service credits

The Supplier will deliver ninety-eight percent of consignments within the agreed delivery window, measured monthly. The window is the one confirmed at the point of booking, and a window changed by the consignee is measured against the revised window.

Failure below ninety-eight percent in a month attracts a service credit of one percent of that month's invoiced charges for each full percentage point below the target, capped at eight percent of the month's charges. Failure below ninety-four percent in any two months within a rolling six-month period gives the company the right to terminate on thirty days' notice without further liability. Service credits are applied automatically against the following month's invoice, without the company having to claim them, and the Supplier reports performance monthly whether or not a credit is due.

## Claims, loss and damage

Loss or damage apparent on delivery is noted on the proof of delivery and notified to the Supplier within three working days. Concealed loss or damage is notified within seven working days of delivery. Claims are submitted in writing within thirty days of notification, with the consignment reference, the value claimed and supporting documentation.

The Supplier's liability for loss or damage is limited to the invoiced value of the goods, or 25 EUR per kilogram of the affected consignment, whichever is lower, except where the loss results from a temperature excursion the Supplier failed to notify, in which case the cap is the invoiced value of the goods without the weight-based limit. The Supplier settles or formally declines a claim within thirty days of receiving complete documentation. The Supplier may not withhold settlement of an undisputed claim on account of a separate commercial dispute.

## Insurance, audit and regulatory compliance

The Supplier holds a wholesale distribution authorisation for medicinal products valid throughout the term, and notifies the company within two working days of any variation, suspension or revocation. It maintains public liability cover of at least ten million EUR and goods-in-transit cover of at least two million EUR per vehicle, evidenced annually.

The company and its customers may audit the Supplier's sites, systems, temperature records and training records on fifteen working days' notice, or without notice following a serious incident. The Supplier responds to audit findings with a corrective action plan within fifteen working days. The Supplier also permits regulatory inspectors access as required by law and informs the company of any inspection affecting the company's consignments within one working day of it being notified or taking place.

## Charges, invoicing and termination

Storage is charged per pallet position per week, in arrears, with part weeks charged as full weeks. Handling and delivery are charged per movement at the rates in schedule B. Fuel is subject to a monthly surcharge calculated from the published national average diesel price against the baseline stated in schedule B.

Invoices are issued monthly and payable within forty-five days of receipt of a valid invoice. The company may withhold a disputed line, having notified the dispute within fifteen days, and pays the undisputed remainder on time. Either party may terminate for material breach unremedied after thirty days' written notice, for insolvency, or for a change of control of the other party that places it under the control of a direct competitor. On termination, the Supplier releases all the company consignments and records within twenty working days, at cost, and does not exercise any lien over them for sums in dispute.
