## Purpose and scope

This handbook describes the terms, expectations and entitlements that apply to everyone employed by the company, including its subsidiaries in Rotterdam, Gdansk and Halifax. It applies to permanent staff, fixed-term staff and interns. Contractors engaged through an agency are covered by their agency's terms, except for the safety rules in section eight, which apply to anyone on a company site.

Where local employment law grants an entitlement more generous than the one described here, local law prevails and this handbook is read down accordingly. Nothing in this handbook forms part of an individual contract of employment unless the contract says so explicitly. People Operations reviews the handbook annually and republishes it after each review. The revision date at the top of this document is the authoritative one, and earlier copies circulating by email or on local drives are not.

## Working hours and attendance

Standard working time is 38 hours per week, normally worked between 07:00 and 19:00 local time. Operations roles attached to a terminal work to a published shift roster issued at least fourteen days in advance. A roster may be changed inside the fourteen day window only with the affected employee's agreement or where a service disruption makes the change unavoidable.

Employees record attendance through the workforce portal. Managers approve timesheets weekly. Overtime is paid at 1.25 times base rate on weekdays and 1.5 times base rate on Sundays and public holidays, and must be approved in advance by the terminal manager. Time off in lieu may be taken instead of payment where both the employee and the manager agree, and must be used within three months of accrual. Persistent unapproved absence is treated under the performance and conduct procedure in section seven.

## Probation and confirmation

New employees serve a probationary period of six months. For roles graded M4 and above the period is nine months. During probation either party may terminate the employment with two weeks' written notice. Managers hold a documented review at the midpoint and again two weeks before the period ends.

Probation may be extended once, by up to three months, where the manager can point to a specific capability gap and a plan to close it. An extension must be agreed by People Operations before it is communicated to the employee. Confirmation is not automatic: a manager who takes no action before the end date leaves the employee confirmed by default, and the company treats that as a management failure rather than as grounds to revisit the confirmation.

## Compensation and pay cycle

Salaries are paid monthly, on the last banking day of the month, covering that calendar month. Where the last banking day falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the preceding banking day. Pay slips are issued electronically through the workforce portal and are retained there for seven years.

Base salary is reviewed annually with effect from 1 April. The review considers market benchmarks for the role and location, individual performance over the preceding twelve months and internal relativity within the grade. A review does not guarantee an increase. Off-cycle adjustments are made only on promotion, on a material change in job scope, or to correct a demonstrated pay equity gap. Variable pay for commercial roles is governed by the separate incentive plan documents issued each year.

## Expenses and company property

Employees may incur reasonable business expenses in line with the travel and expense policy. Laptops, phones, handhelds and vehicle keys issued by the company remain company property and must be returned on the last working day. Equipment lost or damaged through negligence may be recharged, capped at one month's net pay.

Personal use of a company laptop is permitted within the limits of the acceptable use section of the information security handbook. Personal use of a company vehicle is not permitted outside the commute unless the fleet manager has approved it in writing. Fuel cards are issued for business fuel only. The company audits fuel card transactions quarterly and treats fuel drawn outside working patterns as recoverable unless the employee can account for it.

## Leave entitlements

Full-time employees accrue 26 days of annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in their country of employment. Accrual is monthly and pro-rated for part-time employees and for joiners and leavers. Up to five days may be carried into the following year and must be used by 31 March or they lapse.

Requests are made through the workforce portal and need manager approval. Managers may decline a request where the absence would leave a terminal below its minimum safe staffing level, and are expected to give a reason and offer alternative dates. Between 15 November and 5 January leave in operations roles is granted on a rota agreed by the terminal manager in September, so that coverage over the peak season is settled in advance rather than negotiated during it.

## Performance and conduct

Performance is reviewed twice a year, in April and October. A review records what was achieved, how it was achieved, and what support the employee needs. Ratings are moderated across each function to keep them comparable.

Where performance falls short, the manager first raises it informally and records the conversation. If the shortfall continues, a formal improvement plan is opened, lasting between one and three months, with written objectives and a review date. Conduct matters follow a separate three-stage disciplinary procedure: an investigation, a hearing, and a decision, each with the right to be accompanied. Gross misconduct, which includes theft, falsifying records, being impaired at work and deliberate safety breaches, may lead to dismissal without notice at the first instance.

## Health, safety and site rules

Everyone on a company site wears high-visibility clothing and safety footwear outside the office areas. Yard traffic routes are marked and pedestrian crossings are the only permitted way to cross a vehicle lane. Employees must not operate a forklift, reach truck or yard tractor without a current certification held on file.

All accidents, injuries and near misses are reported to the shift supervisor before the end of the shift and logged in the safety system the same day. Near misses matter as much as injuries: they are the cheapest evidence the company gets about a hazard, and the reporting culture depends on them being received without blame. Anyone may stop a task they believe to be unsafe, and no one will be disciplined for stopping a task in good faith.

## Leaving the company

Employees give notice in writing. Notice periods are one month for grades below M4 and three months for M4 and above, unless the individual contract says otherwise. The company may pay in lieu of notice at its discretion.

The leaving employee returns all company property, hands over live work in a documented form and completes an exit interview with People Operations, which is optional but encouraged. System access is revoked at the close of the last working day. Accrued and unused annual leave is paid in the final salary; leave taken beyond accrual is deducted from it. Confidentiality obligations and, where present in the contract, restrictive covenants survive the end of employment and are enforced.
